Wedding season means several events in a few weeks, and each one asks the same question: which earrings? The answer is easier when you decide in the right order. Start with what you are wearing, think about the time of day, and only then pick the pair.

## Start with the outfit, not the earrings

Look at how much is already going on. A heavily worked saree or lehenga has plenty of detail, so simpler earrings let it breathe. A plain or solid-coloured outfit gives you room for a statement pair. One piece should lead and the rest should support it.

## Think about the time of day

- **Daytime events,** such as the haldi, the mehendi or a morning ceremony: lighter pieces and colour work well. A [Minakari](/collections/minakari) pair is a cheerful choice for these.
- **Evening events,** such as the sangeet or the reception: this is where shine and size belong. A [Kundan](/collections/kundan) pair carries well in evening light.

## Match the earrings to the outfit

- **Silk saree:** a Kundan jhumka or drop. The glow suits silk.
- **Lehenga:** a [chandbali](/collections/chandbali) or a large [jhumka](/collections/jhumka). Pull your hair up so the earrings frame your face, and keep the necklace short or skip it.
- **Anarkali or salwar suit:** a medium jhumka or a drop.
- **Light georgette or chiffon saree, or a palazzo set:** Minakari colour, picked up from one shade in the outfit.
- **Indo-western or a jumpsuit:** [hoops](/collections/hoop) or something from our [contemporary](/collections/contemporary) pieces.

Our guide to [Minakari and Kundan](/journal/minakari-vs-kundan-how-to-tell-the-difference) explains what to look for in each, and our guide to [styling jhumkas](/journal/how-to-style-jhumkas) goes further on matching colour.

## Comfort is the part people forget

A wedding day can run from morning to late night. A beautiful pair that feels heavy after two hours will end up in your bag. If you will wear the same pair all day, choose the lighter one, and check the size and weight on the product page where we list them. Try them on at home for twenty minutes before the day. If you notice them, you will notice them at the wedding.

## A few notes on what to avoid

It is the couple's day, so many guests avoid wearing exactly the bride's colour, or all white. That is a matter of local custom and family, so ask if you are unsure. Beyond that, the guide is simple: be festive, and let one piece shine.

## The day-before checklist

1. **Try the whole outfit together,** with your hair and your shoes, and see how the earrings look in a mirror.
2. **Check the closures.** Make sure the backs and hooks are secure.
3. **Pack them in a pouch or box,** each pair separate, if you are travelling.
4. **Put them on last,** after perfume, hairspray and makeup.
5. **Wipe and store them** the same evening with a soft dry cloth. Our guide to [caring for artificial jewellery](/journal/how-to-care-for-artificial-jewellery) has more.
