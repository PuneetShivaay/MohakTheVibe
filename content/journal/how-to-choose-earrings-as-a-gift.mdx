A good pair of earrings is one of the nicest gifts to give: small, personal and easy to wear again and again. It is also easy to get wrong, because taste in jewellery is very personal. This guide gives you a simple way to choose, even when you are not sure what the person likes.

## Start with the person, not the occasion

Before you think about the birthday, the wedding or the festival, think about how the person dresses. Look at what they already wear.

- **Do they wear jewellery every day, or only on occasions?** Everyday wearers want something light. Occasion wearers are happy with more.
- **Do they prefer gold tones or silver tones?** Notice the jewellery they wear most. A gift in the same tone will get worn.
- **Are they minimal, traditional or playful?** This one question narrows the choice more than any other.

## Match the style to the person

- **Minimal:** [studs](/collections/stud) and small [hoops](/collections/hoop). Clean shapes that go with everything.
- **Traditional:** [jhumkas](/collections/jhumka) and [chandbalis](/collections/chandbali), in [Kundan](/collections/kundan) or [Minakari](/collections/minakari) styles.
- **Playful and modern:** [Korean-style earrings](/collections/korean), with their lighter, more contemporary shapes.

If you honestly do not know, studs or small hoops are the safest choice. They suit almost everyone and almost every outfit.

## Think about the occasion

- **Birthdays:** something personal and wearable. Choose the style they would pick for themselves.
- **Weddings and celebrations:** a statement pair, such as a Kundan jhumka or chandbali, that they can wear again at the next occasion.
- **Festivals:** colour. A Minakari pair is an easy, cheerful choice.
- **Thank-you gifts:** a simple, elegant stud or small hoop.

## Gift ideas by person

- **A friend:** a pair that suits her style, in a colour she wears often
- **A sister:** something light and fun, such as a Korean-style pair
- **A mother or elder:** a classic jhumka or a Kundan drop, comfortable and not too heavy
- **A colleague:** a small, polished pair that works for the office

## What to check before you buy

1. **Size and weight.** Heavier pairs are harder to wear for long. Check the details on the product page where they are listed.
2. **Finish.** Gold-tone or another tone, matched to what they already wear.
3. **Style.** One clear style is better than a mix.
4. **Care.** Share our guide to [caring for artificial jewellery](/journal/how-to-care-for-artificial-jewellery) so the gift lasts.

## A note on budget

You do not need to spend a lot. A thoughtfully chosen pair at a modest price will be worn more than an expensive pair that does not suit the person. Spend your effort on getting the style right.

## Making it feel like a gift

Present the earrings in their pouch or box, and add a short note that says why you chose them. A sentence such as "these made me think of you" does more than any wrapping.
