The jhumka is one of the most loved earrings in India, and it is far more versatile than its festive reputation suggests. The bell-shaped dome and the small bell that moves as you do suit a surprising number of outfits. Here is how to wear them, from a plain kurta to a pair of jeans.

## First, how to choose the size

Size decides almost everything about how a jhumka looks.

- **Small jhumkas** sit close to the ear and work from morning to night. They are the easiest way to start.
- **Medium jhumkas** are the all-rounders for festivals, family occasions and dressed-up days.
- **Large jhumkas** are for evenings and celebrations. Let them lead and keep everything else simple.

Weight matters as much as size. A lighter pair is much easier to wear all day, so check the drop length and any weight on the product page where we list it.

## With a kurta

A solid-coloured kurta is the best backdrop for a jhumka, because it gives the earrings room to be seen. Choose a pair that picks up one colour from the outfit, or one that contrasts with it. If your kurta has a high or round neckline, tie your hair up so the earrings are not hidden.

For a printed or embroidered kurta, go smaller or plainer. The outfit is already doing the talking.

## With a saree

A saree can carry a medium or large jhumka well. Look at the blouse: a simple blouse leaves space for a bold pair, while a heavily worked blouse calls for something smaller. With a silk saree, a [Kundan](/collections/kundan) jhumka brings the glow that silk deserves. With cotton or linen, a [Minakari](/collections/minakari) pair adds colour without being too formal.

## With a lehenga

This is where large jhumkas and [chandbalis](/collections/chandbali) belong. Pull your hair up, keep the necklace short or skip it, and let the earrings frame your face. If you are wearing a heavy dupatta, choose earrings with some length so they are not lost in the fabric.

## With western clothes

Jhumkas look good with jeans and a white shirt, which is one of the easiest ways to dress an everyday outfit up. Keep it small or medium, keep the rest of the look minimal, and wear your hair loose or in a simple knot.

A few combinations that work:

- Small jhumkas with a crisp white shirt and straight jeans
- Medium jhumkas with a plain kurti top and a long skirt
- A single bold pair with a black dress, with no other jewellery

## The one-statement rule

Whatever you wear, let one piece lead. If the jhumkas are the statement, skip a heavy necklace. If the necklace is the statement, choose smaller [studs](/collections/stud) or a small jhumka. The outfit always looks better for having a clear focus.

## Matching the colour

Two easy approaches work every time. Tone on tone, where the earrings match the main colour of the outfit, looks polished and quiet. Contrast, such as red enamel against cream or green against pink, looks lively and confident. Gold-tone is the safest finish if you are unsure.
