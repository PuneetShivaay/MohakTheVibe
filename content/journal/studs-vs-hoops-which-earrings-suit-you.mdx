Studs and hoops are the two earrings most people reach for every day. Both are easy to wear and both go with a lot, but they do different jobs. Knowing which job you need done makes choosing much simpler.

## The short answer

Studs are quiet and hoops have movement. If you want a pair you stop noticing after five minutes, choose studs. If you want a small lift around your face, choose hoops.

## Studs: the easy everyday pair

A stud sits flat against the earlobe and stays put. That gives it a few clear strengths:

- **It does not move.** There is nothing to swing, so it is comfortable all day.
- **It is the least likely to catch.** Hair, scarves and jacket collars pass by without a tug.
- **It goes with everything.** A stud adds a little shine without competing with your outfit or your necklace.

Studs suit the office, travel, long days and any time you are wearing something else that is the focus. Our [studs](/collections/stud) come in patterned buttons, crystals and clean shapes, from plain to a little more detailed.

## Hoops: movement and a little more presence

A hoop frames the face and moves as you do, which is what gives it its lift. The size changes the effect completely:

- **Small and slim hoops** hug the ear. They are an everyday pair that feels a step more finished than a stud.
- **Medium hoops** are the classic. They work with a dress, a shirt or a simple kurta.
- **Large hoops** are a statement. Let them lead and keep everything else simple.

Hoops can catch on hair and scarves, and a large pair can feel heavy by the end of a long day, so check the size and weight on the product page where they are listed. Explore our [hoops](/collections/hoop), from slim everyday circles to bold pairs.

## How to choose by outfit

- **A crisp shirt or office wear:** studs or small hoops
- **A printed kurta:** studs, so the print can do the talking
- **A plain kurta:** small hoops, or a small [jhumka](/collections/jhumka) for a more traditional look
- **A dress or western outfit:** medium hoops
- **A saree or lehenga:** neither is the natural choice here. A jhumka or [chandbali](/collections/chandbali) suits it better, and our guide to [styling jhumkas](/journal/how-to-style-jhumkas) shows how.

## How to choose by hair and neckline

Hair changes what you see. With your hair up or cut short, hoops and drops show clearly. With your hair down, studs stay visible while larger hoops can get hidden behind it.

Necklines matter too. A high neckline or a collar sits better with studs or small hoops. A scoop or V neckline gives a medium hoop room to move.

## Can you wear both?

Yes. If you wear more than one pair at once, pair a stud with a small hoop and keep the finish the same. If you are wearing one pair, choose the one that suits your outfit and your day.

## If you are buying only one pair

Start with studs. They are the most versatile, and they are the pair you will reach for most often. Add a pair of medium hoops next, then something with more character for occasions. If you like lighter, more playful shapes, look at our [Korean-style earrings](/collections/korean).
